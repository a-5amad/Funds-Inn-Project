1. How can I take a challenge?

  • Select your desired account size on the Account page and pay the non-refundable service fee for the evaluation.
2. How can I switch between multiple active accounts?

  • Log out and back in with the unique credentials for each account within your supported trading platform (MT4/MT5).
3. How to combine multiple accounts?

  • Account combining is not typically permitted; each challenge is managed separately unless our specific scaling policy is met.
4. How do I request a payout?

  • Payout requests are processed through your client dashboard on a set schedule after qualifying simulated profits are achieved.
5. What is IPU?

  • IPU (Initial Profit Uplift) refers to our high simulated profit split, which is currently up to 90%.
6. What trading platforms do we use?

  • We support industry-standard platforms, including MetaTrader 4 (MT4) and MetaTrader 5 (MT5).
7. Can I buy multiple accounts at the same time?

  • Yes, but you must adhere to our maximum permitted capital allocation limits, as detailed in the Terms of Use.
8. What is the essential guide for beginner traders?

  • The most essential rule is strict Risk Management: focus on preserving capital by adhering to daily and max drawdown limits.
9. Are there any restrictions on trading style?

  • No, we allow most strategies (EAs, hedging, news trading), but prohibited activities (e.g., latency abuse) are strictly enforced.
10. What happens if I fail the challenge?

  • You may purchase a discounted retry or start a new challenge by paying the full service fee again.
